## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

First working version, on localnet.

- Anchor 1.2.1 program: assets with Token-2022 ownership and profit mints (frozen by default,
  transfer hook, metadata), an eligibility gate on Solana Attestation Service attestations, the
  bounty state machine with financial and work bounties, the holding's share on every issuance,
  USDC capital and income vaults with a ledger, and pull distributions whose balances follow every
  transfer through the profit mint's transfer hook.
- The gate thaws only token accounts with `ImmutableOwner`, so a thawed account cannot be handed to
  a wallet that never passed it.
- Council actions through a 2-of-3 Squads v4 vault.
- Kit 8 client generated by Codama from the program IDL and the Squads IDL, with builders that fill
  in every account.
- `npm run dev`: offline Surfpool with the mainnet SAS and Squads binaries, seeded fixtures, and the
  test harness.
- Fixes from an independent review: the distribution remainder is carried in index units, so tiny
  inflows can no longer promise more than they bring; income cannot be recorded before the first
  issuance; the council can free a claimed, undelivered work bounty.
- `revokeAndFreezeInstructions`: revocation and both freezes in one transaction.
- 42 LiteSVM tests, a Surfpool end-to-end test, 6 Rust unit and property tests, and CI with pinned
  actions.
