# Homerun / Founder Haus Token: how the FV mechanism works (EVM, HEAD 5adf3cd, 2026-10-20)

## 1. Summary

Homerun tokenizes one asset (Founder Haus Beach Club, Jurerê Internacional, Florianópolis; docs/FOUNDER_HAUS_SOURCES.md:9-14) with two stock Juicebox-family tokens launched by one singleton, `HomerunDeployer` (0x19Ce092bc3f9662E40C4670C68ff03C8322D0E76 on eight chains; deployments/ethereum/verified.json). FUND is a plain Juicebox V6 project with hardcoded launch rules: 10,000 FUND per USD, a 10% cash-out tax, a USDC treasury, no reserved issuance, no owner minting, no payout limits, a beneficiary allowlist hook, and a zero-duration ruleset so the owner may replace every rule at will (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:681-703). Every FUND goes through the stock omnichain deployer, so its live ruleset data hook is `JBOmnichainDeployer` (with both `useDataHookForPay` and `useDataHookForCashOut` forced to true by that deployer's `_setup721`); `HomerunAllowlistHook` is wired as that deployer's pay-only "extra" hook, and every FUND also carries the omnichain deployer's default 721 tiers hook and the router terminal registry as a second terminal, whether or not it links chains (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:332-333,693-702; test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:499-506,518-523,625-635). A single-chain launch requires `salt == 0`; a linked launch requires a nonzero salt and, on every chain, the same caller, owner, `projectUri`, `name`, `ticker` and `mustStartAtOrAfter`, all of which enter the scoped salt `keccak256(abi.encode(_msgSender(), owner, salt, projectUri, name, ticker, mustStartAtOrAfter))` (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:333-338,366-373, salt at :369; a same-sender launch with other terms lands at different token and sucker addresses, test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:598-623; docs/TRANSACTIONS.md:18). The entire campaign lifecycle (pause, close, success mint, refunds, sale distribution) is not in the contracts: it is a sequence of owner-queued ruleset-metadata edits (`pausePay`, `cashOutTaxRate` 1000/10000/0, `allowOwnerMinting`) that the app builds through `JBOmnichainDeployer.queueRulesetsOf` and the docs describe as manual, non-atomic and reversible (src/lib/fund-contracts.ts:269-291,306-307; docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:36,42; docs/OWNER_ACTIONS.md:36). INCOME is a Revnet with one stage (10 INCOME/USD, 2% cut every 7,884,000 s forever, 10% cash-out tax, owner-chosen reserved percent routed 100% to the `deployIncome` caller) that the FUND owner launches once per FUND; 500,000 INCOME is recorded as a revnet auto-issuance, minted permissionlessly to whoever owns the FUND NFT after the stage starts, and settled to holders off-chain by the owner against an owner-attested snapshot (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:414-458,621-675; docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:25-41). Both contracts are deployed and verified at revision 6e1ea4c (2026-09-24) and confirmed by independent RPC reads on 2026-10-20; several docs saying "undeployed" are stale while others already say deployed (deployments/*/verified.json; reader 4 RPC check; docs/wiring-status.md:9 vs docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:67).

Phase is inferred, not stored: tax 1000 & minting off = raising; tax 10_000 = funded (paused) or earning (INCOME bound); tax 0 = refunding or liquidated (src/lib/fund-phase.ts:19-32).

INCOME side: deployed (auto-issuance recorded, nothing minted) → stage started (`block.timestamp ≥ startsAtOrAfter`) → `mintInitialAllocation` (callable by anyone) triggers the auto-issuance if still pending and transfers the helper's whole INCOME balance to the current FUND owner, once; because `REVOwner.autoIssueFor` is itself permissionless a stranger may mint to the helper first, and INCOME reaching the helper after the sweep is stranded (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:416-419,439-449; test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:477-490) → owner settles off-chain. Chains launched after the shared start inherit REVDeployer's 7-day cash-out delay: `CASH_OUT_DELAY = 604_800`, applied by `_computeCashOutDelayIfNeeded` only when `firstStageConfig.startsAtOrAfter < block.timestamp` (NatSpec at src/HomerunDeployer.sol:267-269; zero delay for a future start at test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:459-463, delay for a past start at :492-496; value in rev-net/revnet-core-v6 REVDeployer.sol). The loan half of the delay is enforced by stock `REVLoans._borrowFrom` (reverts `REVLoans_CashOutDelayNotFinished` while `cashOutDelayOf` is in the future; `borrowableAmountFrom` returns (0, 0) during the delay; rev-net/revnet-core-v6 REVLoans.sol) and is documented at docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:45 and docs/TRANSACTIONS.md:63; no test in the clone exercises it.

## 4. Token economics

**FUND issuance.** `tokens = usd × 10,000` (FUND_WEIGHT 10_000e18, base currency USD, USDC converted via JBPrices) (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:105,689-691). Payment of 1,000 USDC → 10,000,000 FUND (test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:551: 100e6 → 1,000,000e18).

**Cash-out curve.** Stock `JBCashOuts.cashOutFrom`: `reclaim = S·f·[(MAX − t) + t·f]/MAX`, with `f = count/supply`, `t = cashOutTaxRate`, `MAX = 10_000`. At t = 1000: `S·f·(0.9 + 0.1f)` (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:38; web/network-model.mjs:412-413). Worked example, surplus 1,000,000 USDC, holder redeems f = 0.5: gross = 1,000,000 × 0.5 × 0.95 = 475,000 (pro-rata would be 500,000; the curve keeps 25,000 for remaining holders). In the raising state (t = 1000) Juicebox takes the 2.5% protocol fee on the whole reclaim: 475,000 / 40 = 11,875 → net 463,125 USDC (JBConstants STANDARD_FEE 25/1000; JBMultiTerminal `_cashOutTokensOf`, nonzero-tax branch). docs/OWNER_ACTIONS.md:64 supports only that the fee is separate from the tax ("Zero cash-out tax does not eliminate every protocol fee"); the tax-0 case is given under Failure path. Edge cases, in the order `cashOutFrom` checks them: at t = 10_000 the reclaim is 0 without reverting, even for the full supply, because that check precedes the full-supply branch; when `cashOutCount == totalSupply` and the tax is below 100% the whole surplus is returned (a count above supply reverts earlier with `JBTerminalStore_InsufficientTokens` in `JBTerminalStore._computeCashOutFrom`, so the `>=` branch is reachable only at equality); and at t = 0 the result is linear S·f (Bananapus/nana-core-v6 JBCashOuts.sol `cashOutFrom`; JBTerminalStore.sol `_computeCashOutFrom`). The failure-path fork run burns the single holder's entire balance at tax 0, so supply reaches 0 (scripts/verify-fund-fork.mts:269-276).

**Raise goal (demo defaults).** `closingGross = ceil((500,000 + 100,000 + closingCosts) / (1 − 0.025))` = 615,384.62; `raiseGoal = closingGross + precloseSpent` (web/network-model.mjs:175-178; docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:25-34). Investor FUND at full raise: 615,384.62 × 10,000 = 6,153,846,200 FUND (web/network-model.mjs:186). Reaching the goal has no on-chain effect (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:36).

**Success path.** Owner (not operator) mints `mint = I·p/(1−p)`; for p = 20%: I/4 = 1,538,461,550 FUND → total 7,692,307,750, owner 20%. App: `floor((targetBps·supply − 10000·ownerBalance)/(10000 − targetBps))`, any target < 100% accepted; 20% is enforced nowhere on-chain (src/lib/fund-contracts.ts:313-323; docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:42; docs/TRANSACTIONS.md:16).

**Failure path.** Tax set to 0, allowances cleared, optional `addToBalanceOf` of returned cash; each holder reclaims `remainingCash × balance/supply`. No owner FUND, no INCOME (docs/OWNER_ACTIONS.md:62). Fork run: 1,000 USDC contribution, 50 USDC returned, contributor cashes out, supply 0 (scripts/verify-fund-fork.mts:269-276). At tax 0 the 2.5% fee applies only to the part of the reclaim covered by `feeFreeSurplusOf[projectId][token]`, which a terminal increments only from same-terminal project-split payouts received from another project (`executePayout`), same-terminal split pays (`_pay` with an internal split-pay project set) and forgiven fee credits (`_processFee` catch); external `pay` and `addToBalanceOf` do not add to it. Homerun refund and sale cash-outs are therefore fee-free barring those cases, and the `remainingCash × balance/supply` formula holds under that condition (Bananapus/nana-core-v6 JBMultiTerminal.sol `_cashOutTokensOf` zero-tax branch and the three `feeFreeSurplusOf +=` sites).

**INCOME initial allocation and snapshot.** Exactly 500,000e18 across all chains, summed on-chain; per-chain shares recorded as `REVAutoIssuance{beneficiary: deployer}`; snapshot hashes, blocks and per-holder amounts are the owner's attestation (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:119,652-661,705-740). Manifest rules: exact rational FUND weights, global floor division, remainder to lowest (chain, address), zero address kept in denominator, no vesting, no burn, later transfers do not move it (docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:17-21,41; docs/INCOME_RELEASE.md:36; src/lib/fund-global-manifest.ts:136 (sort by chain, address), :158-159 (rational weights, remainder to the first holder), :154-173). Snapshot requires the FUND closed and uses each chain's `finalized` block by default, or an explicit owner-chosen per-chain cut (`input.cuts`) whose block number is at or before `finalized` and whose hash matches (src/lib/fund-snapshot.ts:254; src/lib/fund-global-snapshot-graph.ts:142-168, explicit cut at :147-154). Demo split at 20% owner share: 400,000 investors / 100,000 owner (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:66).

**INCOME issuance.** Live: `weight_q = 10e18 × 0.98^q`, q = floor(elapsed / 7,884,000 s), no floor, forever; proven over 40 quarters (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:110-123; test/HomerunDeployer.t.sol:681-704). After 8 quarters: 8.508 INCOME/USD. Demo (superseded): 5% cut per 3 months for 8 cuts then flat → 6.634204312890625 INCOME/USDC ≈ $0.1507 (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:57-70).

**Splits.** Live: `reservedBps` chosen at launch (UI default 80%), one unlocked split 100% to the `deployIncome` caller; 100 USDC pay → 1,000 INCOME: 800 to the split, 200 to the payer at 8000 bps (test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:335-395; src/HomerunDeployer.sol:641-650). Demo: 75/15/10 operators/holders/customers, `splitPercent 9000`, sub-splits 833333333/166666667 of 1e9 (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:54-56,84). Operator share is a share of issuance, not of cash (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:111).

**INCOME cash-out tax.** 10% live (1000); demo 0%. REVOwner additionally takes 2.5% of the token count (`JBFees.standardFeeAmountFrom(context.cashOutCount)`) as a fee to the fee revnet on ordinary cash-outs only (nonzero cash-out tax, a fee terminal set, beneficiary not fee-less); sucker/bridge cash-outs are quoted at tax 0 and pay neither the tax nor this fee (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:108; docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:60; rev-net/revnet-core-v6 REVOwner.sol `beforeCashOutRecordedWith`). INCOME is launched with `scopeCashOutsToLocalBalances = false`, so cash-outs are quoted against the global, not local, surplus (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:639; test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:818).

**Loans.** Stock REVLoans: collateral burned, loan is an ERC-721, borrowable = cash-out quote of the collateral capped by local surplus (tax acts as implicit haircut), prepaid source fee 2.5–50% (MIN/MAX_PREPAID_FEE_PERCENT 25/500 of MAX_FEE 1000) buying a fee-free window of `prepaidFeePercent × LOAN_LIQUIDATION_DURATION / MAX_PREPAID_FEE_PERCENT` (182.5 days at 2.5%, the full 3650 days only at 50%; REVLoans.sol `_borrowFrom`), 1% $REV fee (REV_PREPAID_FEE_PERCENT 10); after the prepaid window the unpaid source fee on (principal − prepaid) ramps linearly from 0 to 100% at 3650 days (`fee = (t − prepaidDuration) × MAX_FEE / (liquidationDuration − prepaidDuration)`), when the loan liquidates (`REVLoans_LoanExpired`) and can no longer be repaid (rev-net/revnet-core-v6 REVLoansSourceFees.sol `sourceFeeAmountFrom`; line numbers not given because the dependency source is absent from the clone). Demo haircut: net = 94% of principal = "2.5% terminal + 2.5% source + 1% REV" (web/network-model.mjs:88-93). Example: $835.16 cash-out vs $785.05 loan (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:115).

**Sale proceeds.** Net of selling costs and debt plus unused reserve → `addToBalanceOf` → zero-tax cash-outs; owner-held FUND shares pro rata; INCOME treasury never moves to FUND (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:129-133). Fork: 600 USDC returned, three zero-tax cash-outs (scripts/verify-fund-fork.mts:260-264).

**"Mandatory notice delay."** The term appears nowhere in the repo (grep of src, docs, README, web). In Juicebox the primitive is the approval hook `JBDeadline` (a queued ruleset fails unless it starts at least DURATION after queuing; deployed variants 3h/1d/3d/7d). Neither FUND nor INCOME uses it: FUND sets no approval hook and REVDeployer hard-sets it to zero, so owner changes take effect at `mustStartAtOrAfter` (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:686-695; src/lib/fund-contracts.ts:225). ## 8. Risks and open questions

- **Economic.** Owner can rewrite every FUND rule at any time with no notice (zero-duration ruleset); the 20% owner share, refund timing and sale distribution are policy, not code (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:42; docs/OWNER_ACTIONS.md:36). INCOME-only holders have no asset claim; FUND holders' INCOME entitlement is an off-chain promise by whoever owns the FUND NFT at mint time, which may differ from the `deployIncome` caller who holds the operator role and reserved split (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:448,638,646; docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:39). Gap between stage start and `mintInitialAllocation` lets payments/loans occur before the 500k enters supply (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:72 vs INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:31). Swap-routed/buyback payments may skip the reserved split; docs require a controlled path that the contracts do not enforce (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:121-125).
- **Security.** Snapshot is unverified on-chain: a wrong manifest shortchanges holders (docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:27). A 100% tax returns zero rather than reverting, so a non-app client can burn FUND for nothing while funded (docs/OWNER_ACTIONS.md:28). `reservedBps` is not range-checked by HomerunDeployer (src/HomerunDeployer.sol:293-326,667); an out-of-range value reverts downstream in `JBController._queueRulesets` (`JBController_InvalidReservedPercent` above `MAX_RESERVED_PERCENT` 10_000) after REVDeployer copies `splitPercent` into `metadata.reservedPercent`, so it cannot mis-issue, and the app also bounds it (src/lib/income-launch.ts:121); the real gap is that no test covers the >10_000 case. Any FUND ruleset queued without re-supplying the allowlist hook, through `JBController.queueRulesetsOf` directly or through `JBOmnichainDeployer.queueRulesetsOf` with `metadata.dataHook = 0` (any non-Homerun client, or juicebox.money), drops the allowlist gate for the new ruleset (§7). `mintInitialAllocation` assumes INCOME forever has one stage (`latestQueuedRulesetOf`). Allowlist gates payment beneficiaries only (`beforePayRecordedWith`, src/HomerunAllowlistHook.sol:157-169); transfers, bridged mints and owner `mintTokensOf` (success minting) bypass it: `JBController.mintTokensOf` consults no pay hook, only `hasMintPermissionFor` (:173-175, always false, which refuses extra minting rights but does not block owner minting), and the integration setUp mints 300 FUND to ALICE and 100 to BOB without either being allowlisted (src/HomerunAllowlistHook.sol:19-25,157-175; test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol:906-913; Bananapus/nana-core-v6 JBController.sol `mintTokensOf`).
- **Operational.** Docs contradict manifests on deployment status and salt ("HomerunV6" vs keccak("homerun.deployer.global.v4"), docs/INCOME_RELEASE.md:28-29), and docs/INCOME_RELEASE.md:13,23 are stale on the `HomerunDeployerLib` library and the v3 operator argument (§6); fork scripts cannot run against current constructors; test count claims (132 in docs/wiring-status.md:54) do not match the 88 `test*` functions at HEAD (41 in test/HomerunDeployer.t.sol, 22 in test/HomerunDeployer.integration.t.sol, 25 in test/deployment/HomerunDeployment.t.sol); no cross-chain delivery test; no fiat on-ramp; Sticky undeployed. Address precedence is fixed in code: a registry entry wins over the pinned address when present (src/lib/income-contracts.ts:119-129; docs/INCOME_INTEGRATION.md:67; docs/INCOME_RELEASE.md:7); whether nana-sdk-core 3.1.0 (package.json:56) ships a HomerunDeployer/HomerunAllowlistHook entry could not be checked because node_modules is not in the clone.
- **Legal posture (as the docs state it).** A Juicebox token does not establish title; "the corporation" and its governing documents must grant FUND rights; no entity, jurisdiction, price, title or owner is verified (README.md:38; docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:9,19; docs/FOUNDER_HAUS_SOURCES.md:3,40-43). Projections are "bounded scenario results, not guarantees" (docs/NETWORK_DESIGN.md:113).
